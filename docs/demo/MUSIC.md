# Demo music

The Wiretap promo uses a 14.2-second edit of **Night Club Chill** by Alexander
Ehlers (`tricksntraps`). The source track is released under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/t-t-free-cyberpunk-pack-2
- Source file: `night_club_chill_100bpm.ogg`
- Wiretap edit: opening excerpt, short fades, high-pass at 30 Hz, loudness
  normalization, and 48 kHz AAC encoding

Attribution is not required by CC0, but the credit is retained here to preserve
the soundtrack's provenance and make future demo rebuilds straightforward.
